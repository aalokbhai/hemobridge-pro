The problem

In critical moments, finding the right blood type is slow and stressful.

  • No single place to find donors and blood banks
  • Slow communication between patients and donors
  • Hospitals lack simple request tools
  • Donors don’t hear about nearby emergencies

Our solution

One connected platform for donors, patients and hospitals.

  • Nearby blood banks with directions and a call button
  • Alerts to matching donors
  • A control panel for hospitals
  • Recognition for regular donors

Mission and vision

Cut the time it takes to find blood in an emergency, and build a nationwide network where blood unavailability becomes a thing of the past.
